Fleet FAQs

Everything you need to know before choosing the right vehicle for your trip.

What vehicles are available for self-drive and chauffeur services?

We offer economy sedans, family MPVs, and electric vehicles. You can choose a self-drive option or book a driver-assisted ride depending on your needs.

What is included with each rental?

Each rental includes the vehicle, basic insurance, and standard cleaning. Fuel and tolls are typically not included unless specified in your booking.

Can I change my vehicle after booking?

Yes, changes are possible subject to availability. Please contact us as soon as possible so we can arrange the best alternative.

Are electric vehicles available for longer trips?

Electric vehicles are available for local and regional travel. We recommend confirming your route and charging needs in advance so we can support your journey properly.
